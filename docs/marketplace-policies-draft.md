# Marketplace policy drafts for review

**Draft only. Not approved for publication or use as legal advice.** A reviewer must adapt these to each launch country, payment method and delivery model before checkout is enabled.

## Buyer and seller transactions

Buyers and sellers must provide accurate listing and order information. Orders, payment status, refunds, delivery commitments and support decisions must be recorded in the platform. No seller should claim a payment is complete based only on a buyer-provided screenshot. Payment-provider confirmation is authoritative.

## Delivery, returns and disputes

The final policy must state who sets and collects delivery charges, delivery areas and expected times, tracking requirements, failed delivery handling, return eligibility, cancellation cutoffs, refund timing, and how buyers and sellers open and respond to disputes. The current product supports seller-entered fulfillment updates; it does not promise courier scans or delivery guarantees.

## Prohibited items and listing enforcement

The operator must define jurisdiction-specific prohibited and restricted product categories, escalation rules, evidence retention, appeal timelines and emergency removal procedures. Listings remain subject to moderation. The application does not yet contain a legally reviewed prohibited-product catalog.

## Seller verification

The current verification request records a seller's business name, country and supporting text. A verification badge must not be described as identity or business verification until an approved verification method, evidence handling, reviewer process and expiry/recheck schedule are established.

## Privacy and retention

The published notice must explain data categories, purposes, processors, international transfers, retention periods, consent choices, access/export/deletion procedures and exceptions for payments, disputes, fraud and legal obligations. Define response targets and deletion verification before accepting live users.

## Customer support

Publish support hours, channels, expected response times, escalation paths, complaint handling, dispute scope and emergency safety reporting. Assign people to the admin queues and ensure queue aging is monitored.
