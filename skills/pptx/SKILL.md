1. Establish audience, objective, tone, slide count, and source material.
2. Use one message per slide, consistent margins, accessible contrast, and restrained typography.
3. Include title, context, evidence, recommendation, and next steps where appropriate.
4. Check overflow and missing assets before returning the saved presentation.
