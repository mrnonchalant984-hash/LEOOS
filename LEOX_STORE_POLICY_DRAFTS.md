# LeoX Store policy drafts — legal review required

These drafts are product notes, not approved legal terms. Do not publish them as binding terms until counsel adapts them for each operating jurisdiction and confirms the actual payment, delivery, privacy, and dispute processes. Current application code includes buyer/seller, moderation, data-export/deletion-request, consent-record, and notification-preference workflows, but those mechanisms do not themselves constitute an approved legal policy or establish complete compliance.

## Marketplace use and prohibited listings

LeoX Store is a marketplace for buyers and independent sellers. Sellers are responsible for accurate item descriptions, lawful ownership, pricing, delivery promises, and compliance with applicable laws. Listings for illegal goods, stolen property, counterfeit goods, regulated goods offered without required authorization, unsafe goods, or deceptive services may be rejected or removed. LeoX may restrict accounts or listings while investigating a credible safety or fraud report. The final prohibited-item taxonomy and appeal timescale require legal and operational approval.

## Privacy and marketplace data

The Store needs account identifiers, listing details, marketplace messages, reports, moderation decisions, and limited activity data to operate discovery, safety, communications, and support. Public listings and seller profile fields are visible to visitors. Conversation contents and reports are limited to their participants and authorized safety staff. A final notice must state exact processors, legal bases, international transfers, retention periods, user rights, and contact routes before launch.

## Retention, export, and deletion

An export API and reviewed deletion-request workflow exist. They do not automatically erase all marketplace records or storage. Before deletion, the service must remove or anonymize eligible profile, listing, image, saved-product, message, and notification data while retaining only records required for disputes, fraud prevention, safety, or legal obligations. The retention schedule, exceptions, completion deadline, and backup expiry behavior need legal approval. Automated end-to-end deletion and retention enforcement remain incomplete.

## Consent and notifications

Operational messages and optional marketing communications must be separated. Marketing must be off by default and must respect withdrawal. Store notification preferences influence some in-app/email events; push delivery is not connected, and event coverage is incomplete. Consent records exist but do not replace jurisdiction-specific consent capture and withdrawal UX.

## Open review decisions

- Identify the legal entity, marketplace role, governing law, and operating regions.
- Define seller verification, listing appeal, buyer dispute, refund, delivery, and moderation service levels.
- Confirm payment custody and consumer rights before enabling checkout or transaction reviews.
- Approve data categories, processors, cross-border transfers, retention schedule, deletion exceptions, and consent copy.
- Publish accessibility, contact, complaint, and emergency escalation channels.
